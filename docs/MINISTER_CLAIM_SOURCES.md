# Minister claim-to-source / assumption checklist

**Scope:** Independent primary-source verification for issue [L01 — cammeleon66/Cammelot#10](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/issues/10). Covers consequential Dutch policy/legal and sourced national-context claims surfaced on `site/index.html`, `ch0.html`, `ch0-alternative.html`, `ch1.html`, `ch2.html`, `ch3.html`, `about.html`, `minister.html`, `world.html` as checked out at base commit `1de1c45e82832bd707a81185927584fe0b562528`.

**Checked date:** 2026-10-28. **Audit baseline referenced by the issue:** `bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18` (Minister model `minister-care-5-rollout`); current worktree HEAD runs `minister-care-10-access-time-replay` per `docs/MINISTER_MODEL_CONTRACT.md`.

**Method:** Followed each public claim to the primary owning source (Rijksoverheid/VWS, NZa, CBS, RIVM/VZinfo, ABF Research via Rijksoverheid publication, Dutch official programme sites). Dates below distinguish the claim's *original* reference period from its *current* (2026-10-28) status. No simulation rules, Markov transitions, trust/adoption mechanics, or mortality-attribution logic were calibrated or validated — a cited empirical input is never evidence that a game coefficient derived "from" it is itself validated. Rows describe the pre-change baseline; the site changes made in response are listed under *Implementation disposition*.

**This file does not re-derive or endorse any numeric game parameter.** It only checks whether a publicly stated *contextual* fact is supported, current, and accurately worded.

## Summary of high-priority corrections (safe wording; baseline recommendations)

1. **Use NZa's primary definition and scope** (C-01): NR/REG-2421 articles 1(o) and 6 explicitly define the agreed 4-week outpatient/diagnostic and 7-week treatment standards for elective medical-specialist care. The coordinator located and read the regulation after the initial search; do not generalize the game's 12-week rule into law.
2. **The 38% Treeknorm-violation figure is unverified in this pass** — the in-repo citation URL was truncated/unopened and only a secondary newsletter paraphrase was actually read; re-source from the primary NZa monitor or Staat van VenZ page with the exact denominator before republishing (C-02).
3. **Resolve the "strictly factual" vs. "hypothesis test / not clinical evidence" contradiction** on `index.html` (C-15) — this is the exact inconsistency flagged in issue #10's own evidence section.
4. **Flag the 66,400-for-2025 shortfall figure as outdated**; keep 301,000-for-2035 (re-confirmed by the official 19 Dec 2025 Rijksoverheid release) but note the mitigated-scenario figure (227,000) exists in the same official release (C-09).
5. **Remove or re-source the "34% / IZA-Vilans" AI-productivity attribution** (C-11) — no corroborating primary document found; this is model assumption, not sourced external estimate.
6. **Mark MRI/CT 28.9% and specialty-specific +50%-since-2022 figures unverified** (C-03, C-04) pending a located dated NZa/VZinfo series.
7. **Date the IZA announcement accurately** (C-05, C-06): the notice announcing July 2025 closure reports €2.2B available against nearly €3B in plans at that time. It is not today's balance, not disbursement evidence, and not validation of the unreconfirmed €2.8B attribution.
8. **State explicitly that the Minister role/town/budget is a fictional, scaled abstraction**, not a literal national financing model (C-16) — required by the issue's acceptance criteria but not found explicit on the reviewed pages.
9. **Add an on-page legacy/unsupported label to `world.html`** results (C-17), matching what `MINISTER_MODEL_CONTRACT.md` already states at the doc level.
10. **Do not state that the ch1–ch3 comparison statistics "came from" the traced `world.html`-extracting research runner** — this check only traced the runner and one unversioned, undated-to-figures config (2026-06-09); it did not reconcile that artifact against the published chapter numbers. State provenance as unconfirmed/not reconciled, not as an established source (C-18).
11. **Re-check current NZa GP tariff figures** against the 2026-06-30 tariff revision before re-publishing specific euro amounts as current (C-14).
12. **Cite Wegiz as BWBR0048095 wherever it is referenced** and keep Wegiz (a framework law) clearly distinct from FHIR (a data standard) wherever both are listed (C-19).
13. **Label approximate cost proxies (€150/€850/€5,845), fictional per-agent GP/specialist capacities, and un-footnoted in-game "historical fact" reveals (Code Black, medicine-shortage statistics, named ED closures) as unverified/assumptions**, distinct from directly NZa-tariff-coded figures (C-20, C-21, C-23); re-confirm the Chen et al. OR 2.46 figure directly from the PubMed abstract and document its transformation into the in-game comorbidity multiplier (C-22).
