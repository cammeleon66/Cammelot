# Minister model contract

**Status:** Proposed; maintainer approval is still required for the decisions called out below. **Audited baseline:** [`bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18`](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/commit/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18). **Executable model:** `minister-care-5-rollout`. **Paired-reference protocol:** `no-discretionary-action-v1`.

This contract describes the Minister policy game only. It records verified behavior separately from proposed rules; it does not calibrate the model, approve clinical semantics, or authorize a runtime change. The audit summary and browser evidence in issue [#1](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/issues/1) are the supplied evidence; no private audit artifacts are assumed.

## Verified current behavior

### Authority, public modes, and hosting

- The authoritative Minister executable is the inline simulation and policy code in [`site/minister.html`](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html). It runs the care model in the browser. The two cards exposed by the public picker are **Cabinet Crisis** (`cabinetcrisis`, 8 quarters) and **The Full Term** (`campaign`, 40 quarters). They share the same executable and care rules, with different scenario initial conditions and horizons; they are not IST/SOLL toggles. See [scenario and clock definitions](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L1419-L1501) and [picker regression coverage](../tests/minister_browser.test.js#L45-L87).
- `sprint`, `zeeland`, `codeblack` and `sandbox` remain addressable in source as legacy hash scenarios, but are not public picker modes and are not covered by this support contract.
- The release-status record identifies [cammelot.org/minister.html](https://cammelot.org/minister.html) as the public game and reports GitHub Pages/CI and the optional Azure App Service leaderboard as operational. The supported public game path is therefore the static Minister page on that site. The leaderboard is a separate optional service; it is not part of clinical execution. See [release status](MINISTER_RELEASE_STATUS.md) and [leaderboard operations and limitations](COMMUNITY_LEADERBOARD.md).
- Local HTTP serving is a documented development path (`python -m http.server` in the [README](../README.md)); the browser tests also serve the static `site/` files over local HTTP. It does not provide the public leaderboard service. Docker/nginx is documented, but is not established here as a currently release-verified Minister hosting path. `file://` behavior and other hosts are unverified.
- `site/world.html` is a separately implemented, publicly linked standalone simulation. The modular `src/` backend is a separate implementation and does not execute Minister care. Neither World nor backend results validate Minister results; their public support status and deployments are outside this contract. Older architecture descriptions are historical context, not evidence of Minister execution ([ADR-001](ADR.md#adr-001-frontend-first-architecture-all-simulation-logic-in-v4html-no-bundler), [README](../README.md)).

### Time, state, and units

- A logical `cycle` is an integer simulation tick; the Minister policy calendar uses **4 ticks per week**, **52 ticks per quarter**, and **208 ticks per 4-quarter year**. Disease progression is also evaluated every fourth cycle. Tick execution is fixed-step and separate from rendering, pausing, and reading time; wall-clock speed controls how quickly ticks are run, not their model duration. See [clock constants](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L1483-L1486), [tick](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7237-L7249), and [regression checks](../tests/minister_browser.test.js#L89-L151).
- The initialized `cycle` is **30**, not zero. The calendar boundary is calculated from absolute cycle (`floor(cycle / 52) + 1`), so the first displayed quarter is shorter than 52 advances. This offset is not currently normalized into an explicit elapsed-term clock. The annual background-pressure update also uses absolute cycles.
- Current model state uses health points (HP; nominally 0–100), age in years, ordinal disease severities, waiting time in weeks, capacity in routine treatment starts per week, budget in euros, trust/adoption/absence as fractions or percentage-like scores, AI/drain as multipliers, and scenario/horizon in integer quarters. Existing source does not provide a single validated parameter schema or upper bounds for every control. Observed starting values include IST admin 0.30, staff absence 0.08, norm 12 weeks, AI multiplier 1.0, drain multiplier 1.0; SOLL values are 0.05, 0.05, 4 weeks, 1.34 and 0.30 respectively. Minister starts with IST policy state; do not infer that the standalone World mode switch is active in this game. See [parameter defaults](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L1825-L1833).
- The code's background mortality calculation uses `48/5000/(365*4)` per tick and labels its divisor “4 ticks/day”; its age multipliers are 1.5, 5 and 15 at ages 45, 65 and 80. Resident ages are not advanced by the Minister tick. This differs from the 4-tick/week policy clock and is not a validated annual mortality implementation. Do not present the displayed result as calibrated annual mortality. See [background mortality and disease progression](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7338-L7352).

### Care resources, waits, and deaths

- `specialistCapacity` is a nominal rate of **routine treatment starts per week**, not beds, available staff headcount, completed treatments, or realized throughput. Routine admission starts accrue service credit per tick; the current helper also limits them by an occupancy-like `staffedSlots = ceil(rate × 2.5)` (minimum one) less the count already receiving hospital care. Started treatment has its own simulated duration. The number actually started is realized throughput and can be below the nominal rate due to demand, occupancy, or integer credit. See [capacity helper](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L12501-L12535) and [routine admissions](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L8012-L8061).
- A tracked referral episode has a start and end tick and currently ends as `treatment_started`, `died_waiting`, `relocated`, or `other_exit`. The live “waiting” snapshot includes patients travelling to hospital, emergency patients, and those in the hospital queue; it is not simply queue length. In particular, an emergency episode can have zero displayed wait. These episode counts are model bookkeeping, not a complete measure of access.
- Critical/low-HP patients can enter the emergency pathway and bypass the hospital queue, going directly to treatment. This path is not charged against `availableStarts` or the routine occupancy count. No finite emergency resource/overflow consequence is represented in this path. See [emergency trigger](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7392-L7412) and [emergency admission](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7828-L7844).
- A `system_failure` grave marker means the model assigned the system-failure cause when HP reached zero, including a terminal disease transition for selected conditions. A `natural` marker is assigned by the separate background hazard. The result UI calls the first category “model-classified system deaths”; neither label establishes real-world causation or preventability. “Deaths before treatment” counts tracked referral episodes ending in death, not all system-classified deaths. See [death handling](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7278-L7307), [disease terminal states](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7380-L7387), and [paired result wording](MINISTER_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.md#matched-date-results).
- The executable declares model `minister-care-5-rollout` and paired-reference protocol `no-discretionary-action-v1`. Matched snapshots require matching model, scenario, seed, and tick; reference data also checks protocol and schedule identity. Previous-attempt local storage is keyed by model/scenario/seed; leaderboard requests include model version and scenario; old-version links warn that the current town cannot reproduce the older outcome. This warning does not provide an old executable. See [version and snapshot checks](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L12501-L12575), [previous-attempt record](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L12667-L12673), and [leaderboard filtering](COMMUNITY_LEADERBOARD.md#public-data).

### Inputs, rules, controls, and outputs

- **Empirical context:** project documentation attributes population, disease, mortality, tariff, and workforce context to sources including CBS, RIVM, NZa and IZA. A source label alone does not make a game coefficient calibrated. Each empirical input used quantitatively should have a traceable source, reference date, transformation, unit, and uncertainty.
- **Assumptions:** the 45 named-resident implementation, scenario starting values and horizons, age multipliers, disease transitions, the 12-week policy norm, routine capacity/occupancy algorithm, deterministic shock schedule and shock effects are model assumptions or gameplay abstractions unless separately evidenced. The [paired-reference protocol](MINISTER_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.md#shared-environment) explicitly labels its shock coefficients and schedule as assumptions.
- **Authored policy rules:** interventions, laws, eligibility, adoption scaling, political thresholds, and effects in the Minister executable are game rules, not empirical findings. For example, recruitment increases the routine start rate; scribes change admin burden; law effects are applied through the same policy-effect mechanism. See [representative intervention definitions and application](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L12774-L12857) and [law enactment](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L14480-L14499).
- **Measured outputs:** tick-indexed counts and summaries (e.g. queue/episode state, treatment starts, deaths by model classification, HP, trust, budget, admin and burnout) describe this run under this executable and seed. Seeded repeatability is a software property, not clinical validation, causal attribution, or evidence that every beneficially described intervention must improve every outcome.
- Current executable paths show admin burden affecting GP queue blocking and overdue-wait HP drain; the referral norm affects deterioration and norm reporting; drain multipliers affect passive disease drain; routine capacity affects routine starts. The aggregate sick-leave percentage is displayed and changed by policies/shocks, while individual GP timers gate some GP queue service; the aggregate percentage is not itself the timer/queue gate. The AI multiplier is included in displayed effective-capacity arithmetic, but it is not the routine hospital start-rate control. Recruitment changes starts/week. See [HP drain and policy rule](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L4248-L4283), [GP queue and capacity path](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L7978-L8017), and [effective policy scaling](https://github.com/cammeleon66/Cammelot/blob/bb6df6490ceb56e68cc8922ae167660ac6079b18/site/minister.html#L12903-L12923). Exact intended clinical mappings for workforce/productivity controls still need approval; see below.

## Proposed contract rules

These are recommendations for maintainer approval, not claims about current behavior or approved clinical choices.

1. **One executable per declared model version.** The Minister page is authoritative for Minister outcomes. Cabinet Crisis and Full Term are supported scenarios under that executable; their initial state and horizon are part of the scenario identity. World and backend remain separately identified products and must not be silently substituted.
2. **Explicit units and input validation.** Persist units with every model parameter. Require finite nonnegative tick counts, waits, costs and rates; HP and fractional probabilities/absence/admin/adoption in `[0,1]`; positive finite multipliers; integer positive scenario quarter counts; and enumerated disease-state/severity values. A value outside its declared range must be rejected or surfaced, never silently coerced. These are proposed schema constraints, not validation guarantees in the audited source.
3. **Keep resource measures distinct.** Declare nominal capacity as starts per week; report realized treatment starts separately; define occupancy as patients actually receiving care; and report queue, travel, treatment, and discharge states separately. Any capacity change must state which service and resource it affects.
4. **Do not overstate outcome labels.** Use “model-classified system deaths” unless/until domain review approves a stronger label. Report referral deaths separately. Waiting time must name its episode, start/end event, clock, and whether the metric is current-queue, episode, or completed-wait time.
5. **Tie productivity controls to named processes.** An intervention advertised as changing admin, absence, workforce, or AI productivity must identify the affected provider/service process and an observable intermediate measure (for example, GP service slots, GP queue service, routine specialist starts, or task time). A displayed effective-capacity index alone is not realized throughput. No clinical benefit is presumed; trade-offs and mixed outcomes remain valid.
6. **Immutable version identity.** A model version changes for any outcome-affecting change to initialization, clock interpretation, scenario, parameters, disease transitions, resource/overflow rules, intervention/law mapping, death/wait definitions, random schedule/RNG, or score/outcome semantics. UI-only changes do not require a model bump. Change the paired-reference protocol version when its policy or comparison contract changes. Publish the exact source revision with each release.
7. **Keep incompatible records separate.** A result identity includes model version, scenario, seed, and ending tick; paired comparisons also include protocol and matching schedule/reference identity. Do not merge rankings across model versions or compare unequal ending dates. Preserve old-version leaderboard entries as separately labeled/filterable records. Replay/previous-attempt data must retain model version, scenario, seed, protocol, ending tick, and recorded decisions; label it non-replayable if its executable is unavailable. Current records are version-scoped, and “Try another approach” is a previous-attempt comparison rather than automatic re-execution of the recorded choices ([reference protocol](MINISTER_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL.md#replay), [leaderboard contract](COMMUNITY_LEADERBOARD.md#public-data)).

### Emergency overflow: decision requested

**Recommendation:** represent emergency demand explicitly against a finite, declared emergency resource; when that resource is full, keep the person in a visible, priority-ordered overflow state and record the wait, resource occupancy, outcome, and any displacement of routine care. Do not silently admit unlimited emergencies, silently cap/deny them, or infer that overflow itself causes a particular clinical outcome. No resource count, priority rule, overflow clinical consequence, or numerical coefficient is approved by this document.

Alternatives considered: (a) prohibit overflow and deny admission at the cap, which is simple but implies a consequential access rule; (b) queue overflow without a finite resource/outcome model, which preserves access bookkeeping but does not model pressure; or (c) explicitly represent finite emergency capacity and overflow consequences, which is auditable but requires domain review and additional state. Option (c) is the bounded recommendation; maintainer and clinical-domain approval is required before implementation. Until then, current unlimited queue-bypass behavior is documented, not endorsed as the contract.

## Glossary

- **Model version:** immutable identifier for a particular outcome-affecting model and policy implementation.
- **Protocol version:** identifier for the paired-reference policy and comparison rules.
- **Nominal capacity:** declared service rate before demand and resource constraints are applied.
- **Realized throughput:** number of services actually started or completed during a stated interval.
- **Occupancy:** number of patients using a stated care resource at a stated time.
- **Waiting episode:** one named interval between a defined wait-start event and treatment or another defined exit.
- **Model-classified system death:** a death assigned to the model's system-failure category; not proof of preventability or real-world cause.
